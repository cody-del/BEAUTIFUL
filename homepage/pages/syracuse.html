Tell us when sunlight or privacy is a concern. We compare fabrics against your view and discuss coverage for the evening.

Solar shades for lake-facing windows

Solar mesh can soften glare and reduce UV exposure while retaining a daytime view. For windows facing the water, compare fabric openness in the actual light. We can show how a tighter weave differs from a more open one. After dark, mesh may allow visibility into a lit room, so plan another covering where privacy is needed.

Explore solar shades

Cellular shades for year-round rooms

Cellular fabric uses air pockets to help insulate at the window. Compare light-filtering and room-darkening choices for bedrooms and living spaces used through the colder months. We also check how much of the window the folded fabric covers when raised. If keeping the view clear is a priority, mounting position and stack height should be part of the decision.

Compare cellular shades

Roller shades for guest rooms and living areas

Roller shades offer a simple profile and fabric choices from light-filtering to blackout. A guest bedroom may need opaque coverage, while a living area may benefit from softer daylight. Tell us about early sun or outside lighting that affects sleep. We check the mounting and side coverage because blackout fabric alone does not seal every gap.

Roman shades and plantation shutters

Roman shades add fabric texture and folds, with lining choices to compare for the room. Shutters offer a framed finish with adjustable louvers. We check how either treatment works with handles, trim, and furniture. For a seasonal or humid space, discuss the conditions before selecting fabric, wood, or composite materials.

Will a raised shade leave the whole window clear?

The roll, fabric stack, or headrail still takes up space. We can compare product profiles and mounting positions if keeping more glass visible is important to you.

Do lake-facing windows need different fabric?

They may need a different balance of glare control, view, and privacy. Seeing samples at the window helps you judge the choice. We also ask about room humidity and whether you heat and cool the room year-round.

Can I use one remote for several shades?

Compatible motorized systems can offer grouped controls. We will check that the treatments and motors you choose work together and explain any accessories needed for additional features.
