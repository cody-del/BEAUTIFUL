Solar fabrics for bright views

If you have lake-facing glass or a sunroom, compare solar fabric openness at the actual window. Screen color and weave affect both the view and glare control. Solar shades are not a complete nighttime privacy solution; plan a separate layer if the room needs one.

Compare solar shade fabrics

Roller shades for privacy and bedrooms

Light-filtering and room-darkening fabrics give a roller shade different uses. In a bedroom, check the mounting position and light around the edges as well as the fabric itself. A blackout fabric does not automatically make the whole room dark.

Explore roller shades

Cellular shades for window comfort

Cellular construction traps air in pockets at the window. Compare cell sizes and layers, opacity, and the space the shade occupies when raised. Performance depends on the selected product and fit, so choose from the specifications and samples rather than an assumed bill saving.

Explore cellular shades

Roman shades for texture and pattern

Roman shades add fabric folds to a dining room, bedroom, or living area. Compare compatible linings and the amount of glass covered by the raised stack. This matters when preserving an open view is part of your plan.

Older windows and original trim

Measure the recess and check uneven openings before selecting hardware. We discuss how much trim stays visible and where an outside mount may be useful.

Wide glass and patio access

Look at how the treatment lifts or draws and where it stacks. A window covering beside a patio door needs to leave handles and walking space accessible.

Open rooms and high windows

Decide which windows should coordinate and which need a different opacity. Discuss control access and power while planning treatments for high or hard-to-reach glass.

Can I reduce glare and still see outside?

A solar screen may help, depending on its fabric and the light at your window. Compare openness and color with samples. If you also need privacy after dark, discuss an additional layer or another shade style.

Do all the windows need the same shade?

No. You can coordinate colors while using different fabrics or controls for different rooms. A bedroom's privacy and darkness needs may differ from those of a living room or office.

Can we arrange a visit when I am at the property?

Give us your address and available dates when you contact us. We will confirm coverage and appointment availability. Order and installation timing are discussed separately once you select the products.

Do you measure and install the treatments?

Yes. We measure for the custom order and arrange installation when it arrives. We also check the operation and explain how to use the selected controls.
