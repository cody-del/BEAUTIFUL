For large windows and whole-home projects, start with what each space needs, then bring the finishes together.

Cellular shades for comfort

Cellular shades use air pockets within the fabric to help insulate at the window. Compare single- and double-cell designs, light-filtering and room-darkening fabrics, and controls. We can explain how the options compare at your windows. The fabric, fit, and existing window all affect the result.

Compare cellular shades

Roman shades for living spaces

Roman shades add a soft fabric finish in bedrooms, dining rooms, and living rooms. Compare flat and soft-fold styles and the available linings. The raised folds occupy space at the top of the window, so mounting position and the view matter as well as the pattern.

See Roman shade options

Roller and solar shades for larger windows

Roller shades offer a simple profile with a choice of fabrics. For strong daylight, compare solar screen openness and glare control. For bedrooms, discuss room-darkening fabric and edge coverage. Solar mesh may let people see into a lit room after dark, so we discuss nighttime privacy separately.

Explore roller shades

Make high windows easier to use

Before ordering for several rooms

A coordinated home does not require the same fabric on every window. These decisions help keep the project practical.

Start with priority rooms

You can begin with bedrooms and main living areas, then plan other spaces later. Note where people can see in from the street, where evening light affects sleep, and where glare reaches a screen. Compare those needs alongside your budget.

Check the finished openings

New trim, paint, and window hardware can affect mounting depth and clearances. Tell us about work still to be completed. We take the measurements used for the order and explain when a final measurement is needed.

Compare shutters and blinds

Shutter frames become a visible part of the opening, while blinds provide tilting slats with a different profile. Compare wood and composite shutters or wood and faux wood blinds with your trim. Check panel clearance, material care, and operation before deciding.

Can we plan window coverings before move-in?

Yes. Contact us with your construction timeline and the rooms you want to cover. We can discuss choices and arrange final measurements when the windows and mounting surfaces are ready. We’ll let you know how long your order is expected to take.

Do I have to order for the whole house?

No. Start with the rooms that matter most to you. We can help you plan the project in stages and find finishes that work together.

Will motorized shades work with my smart-home system?

Compatibility depends on the motor, controls, and accessories. Tell us which system you use. We’ll check which shades and controls work with your setup before you order.
