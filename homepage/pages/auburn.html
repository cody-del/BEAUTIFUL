The original treatment may no longer suit the room. Think about when glare is strongest, where you need privacy, and which windows are awkward to reach.

Cellular shades and window comfort

Cellular shades use honeycomb pockets to trap air at the window. Compare single- and double-cell construction, how much light the fabric blocks, and the fit. They are worth considering when comfort is a priority, but the result depends on the window, product, and installation.

Compare cellular shades

Solar and roller shades

For a home office or bright living room, compare solar screen fabrics for glare control with some outward view. Plan separately for nighttime privacy. Roller fabrics offer light-filtering and room-darkening choices; blackout fabric alone does not eliminate light around the edges.

Roman shades and fabric finishes

Roman shades gather into folds as they rise. Compare flat and soft-fold styles, texture, pattern, and compatible linings. We also check how much glass the raised fabric will cover and which care instructions apply to the material you choose.

Fitting the windows in your Auburn home

The opening matters more than the age or style of the house. We check the details before your custom treatment is ordered.

Existing trim and unusual openings

Older windows may have shallow recesses, uneven openings, or trim you want to keep visible. We check mounting depth and hardware clearance, then explain inside- and outside-mount options.

Picture windows and patio doors

Wide glass needs a practical way to lift, tilt, or draw the treatment. We look at the weight, where the treatment stacks, and how you use the door. Furniture and handles need clearance too.

New construction and whole-home plans

Bring window coverings into the planning conversation before move-in. We can compare room priorities and discuss when openings are ready for final measurements. We’ll let you know when to expect your treatments and installation.
