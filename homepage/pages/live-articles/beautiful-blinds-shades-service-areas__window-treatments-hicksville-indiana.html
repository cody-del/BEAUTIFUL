Blinds, Shades, and Window Treatment Solutions for Hicksville, Ohio Homes

Hicksville winters can be brutal, and heating costs add up quickly. Cellular shades create insulating air pockets that keep your home warmer when it's cold and cooler during those humid summer months. We've installed these in homes throughout Hicksville, and homeowners consistently tell us they notice the difference in both comfort and utility bills.

Double-cell options work especially well for rooms that always seem cold or for older homes with original windows. If you're in one of Hicksville's classic homes, cellular shades can make a real difference without the major expense of window replacement.
