Window treatments that suit Monroeville homes

Every house is different, so we start by asking how you use each room: which windows get the hot afternoon sun, which rooms feel drafty in winter, and where you want privacy without losing the view.

Cellular shades for drafty rooms

Cellular shades trap air in honeycomb-shaped cells, which slows heat moving through the glass. They are a good fit for bedrooms and living rooms that feel cold in January or warm in July, and they come in light-filtering and room-darkening fabrics.

Blinds for everyday rooms

Custom blinds in wood, faux wood or aluminum give you precise light control with a turn of the wand. Faux wood holds up well in kitchens, bathrooms and laundry rooms where humidity would warp real wood.

Solar and roller shades for big views

Homes with wide windows facing fields or a long yard often deal with glare. Solar shades cut glare and heat while keeping the view, and roller shades give a clean, simple look in any room.

Plantation shutters are mounted to the window frame and stay with the house. Adjustable louvers let you control light and privacy, and composite shutters handle bathrooms and kitchens where moisture is a concern.

Sliding doors and patios

For patio doors, vertical blinds and panel track blinds slide to the side so the door stays easy to use. For a porch or patio, outdoor shades block afternoon sun and add privacy.

Motorized options

Shades on tall or hard-to-reach windows can be motorized, so you can raise and lower them with a remote or on a schedule instead of reaching over furniture.

Repair and cleaning for the blinds you already have

Not every window needs something new. If a cord has snapped, a shade will not stay up, or slats are bent, our blind and shade repair service can often fix it. We also offer blind and shade cleaning to remove built-up dust and grime.
