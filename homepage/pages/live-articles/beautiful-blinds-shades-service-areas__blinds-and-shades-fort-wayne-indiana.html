Understanding Fort Wayne's Housing Market

Fort Wayne's housing market is incredibly diverse, reflecting the city's growth from its founding to present day. You'll find everything from beautifully preserved Victorian homes to brand-new construction, each with different window treatment needs.

Historic Neighborhoods: Areas like West Central, Williams Woodland Park, and Lakeside feature stunning older homes with original architectural details and unique window configurations. Custom treatments ensure proper fit while enhancing character and improving energy efficiency.

Mid-Century Neighborhoods: Established areas throughout Southwest Fort Wayne, around Georgetown, and near State Boulevard feature solid, well-built homes typically from the 1950s-1980s. These benefit from updated treatments that improve comfort and appearance.

Downtown and Near-Downtown: Revitalized lofts, condos, and renovated properties bring urban living to Fort Wayne. These often feature large windows and industrial elements that work beautifully with modern window treatments.

Growing Corridors: Areas near the medical corridor, along Dupont Road, and in developing sections of the city feature contemporary homes that benefit from smart, energy-efficient window solutions.
