Types of roman shade styles

The fold style changes the entire look. Here are the most popular:

Flat roman shades - Smooth fabric when lowered, clean horizontal folds when raised. Most contemporary and minimal look. Works everywhere.

Hobbled (soft fold) roman shades - Fabric cascades in soft, relaxed folds even when lowered. More traditional, elegant appearance. Beautiful in dining rooms and bedrooms.

Relaxed roman shades - The bottom curves into a gentle swag instead of lying flat. Softer, more casual feel. Popular in kitchens and informal spaces.

Waterfall roman shades - Cascading layers that create dramatic visual impact. Makes a statement in living rooms or anywhere you want wow factor.

Fabric choices matter

The fabric you choose affects both how the shade looks and how it performs.

Light-filtering fabrics - Natural weaves, sheers, semi-opaque materials. They soften daylight, provide daytime privacy, but won't darken the room. Most popular for living areas.

Room-darkening fabrics - Heavier, denser materials that significantly reduce light. Good for bedrooms but not total blackout without lining.

Blackout lining - Can be added to almost any fabric. Blocks 99% of light for serious darkness in bedrooms or media rooms.

Patterns vs solids - Patterns add personality and can hide minor cleaning needs. Solids offer timeless elegance and work with changing decor.

Texture - Linen weaves, woven patterns, and textured fabrics add depth. Smooth fabrics feel more formal and contemporary.

Your lifestyle matters too. Have kids or pets? Darker colors and patterned fabrics hide wear better. Want easy care? Skip silk blends and delicate materials.

Lining makes a difference

Most roman shades benefit from lining, even if you don't need blackout.

Standard lining - Protects fabric from sun damage and fading. Adds body to the shade so it hangs better. Makes the shade last longer.

Blackout lining - Blocks light completely. Essential for bedrooms where you need darkness. Also provides better insulation.

Thermal lining - Helps with energy efficiency. Reduces heat gain in summer, keeps warmth in during winter. Not as common as blackout but worth considering for problem windows.

Unlined roman shades work for casual spaces where you want maximum light filtering, but lining generally improves both function and longevity.

What to know about roman shades

They need more maintenance than roller shades. The fabric and folds collect dust. Regular vacuuming with a brush attachment keeps them clean. Some fabrics can be spot cleaned, others need professional cleaning.

They're more expensive than basic options. Quality fabric and construction costs more. But you're getting a premium look that elevates your entire room.

Cord safety matters. Traditional roman shades use cords and rings on the back. For homes with young children, choose cordless lift or motorization to eliminate cord risks.

They stack higher than roller shades. When fully raised, roman shades take up more window height due to the fabric folds. Plan for about 8-12 inches of stack depending on window size.

Professional installation is worth it. Roman shades need to hang properly for smooth operation and the right aesthetic. Poor installation shows immediately.

Common questions

How much do roman shades cost?Roman shades typically cost more than roller shades due to fabric and construction. Pricing depends on window size, fabric choice, lining, and features. We provide transparent pricing during your consultation.

Are they hard to clean?They need more maintenance than roller shades but aren't difficult. Regular dusting or vacuuming prevents buildup. Some fabrics handle spot cleaning, others need professional care. We'll guide you based on your fabric choice.

Can I get blackout roman shades?Absolutely. Add blackout lining to almost any fabric for complete darkness. Perfect for bedrooms where light control is essential.

Do they provide good insulation?With thermal lining, yes. The fabric plus lining creates a barrier that helps with temperature control. Not as effective as cellular shades but better than basic roller shades.

Which fold style is most popular?Flat roman shades are the most versatile and contemporary. Hobbled (soft fold) is popular for traditional homes. It really depends on your decor style.

Are they safe for kids?Choose cordless lift or motorization to eliminate cord hazards. Traditional corded roman shades have cords on the back that can pose risks.

How long do they last?Quality roman shades with proper care last many years. The fabric may fade over time with sun exposure, but good lining protects against that. The mechanism should operate smoothly for a long time.

Can I wash them?Most roman shade fabrics should not be washed at home. Spot cleaning works for minor issues. For deep cleaning, professional service is safest to protect the fabric and structure.
