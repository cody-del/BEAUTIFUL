Why solar shades make sense

You keep your view. This is the big one. Solar shades use mesh fabric that blocks sun and heat while you still see your backyard, garden, or neighborhood. No more choosing between comfort and your view.

Serious UV protection. Block up to 99% of harmful UV rays that fade furniture, flooring, and artwork. Your home's interior stays vibrant longer.

Heat reduction. South and west-facing windows can turn rooms into ovens during Indiana summers. Solar shades significantly reduce heat gain before it enters your home.

Glare control. Watch TV, work on your computer, or just relax without harsh sunlight in your eyes. Solar shades cut glare while maintaining natural brightness.

Daytime privacy. The mesh fabric works like a one-way mirror during daylight - you see out clearly, but people outside see mostly reflection. At night when lights are on inside, the effect reverses.

Energy savings. By blocking heat before it enters your home, your AC doesn't work as hard. Real savings on summer cooling costs.

Understanding openness factors

Solar shades come in different openness percentages - basically how tight the weave is. This matters more than most people realize.

1% openness - Tightest weave. Blocks 99% of UV rays and provides maximum heat control. View is slightly filtered, like looking through a fine screen. Best for windows with intense sun exposure.

3% openness - Most popular choice. Blocks 97% of UV rays with good heat control. Clear outward view during the day. Great balance for most homes.

5% openness - Looser weave. Blocks 95% of UV rays with the clearest view. Less heat control but maximum visibility. Works well for north-facing windows or shaded areas.

10% openness - Very open weave. Blocks 90% of UV rays. Brightest option with excellent view. Better for areas where heat isn't a major concern.

During your consultation, we'll help you pick the right openness based on which direction your windows face, how much sun they get, and what matters most to you.

Motorized solar shades

Manual operation works fine, but motorization makes sense for solar shades because you're adjusting them based on sun position throughout the day.

Automated schedules. Program shades to lower when afternoon sun hits certain windows, then raise later when sun moves.

Smart home integration. Control through Alexa, Google Home, or your phone. Adjust multiple shades at once with a voice command.

Hard-to-reach windows. High windows, wide expanses, or windows behind furniture become easy to operate.

Battery-powered motors mean no electrician needed. They run for months on a charge and operate whisper-quiet.

Common questions

How much do solar shades cost?Depends on window size, fabric choice, and features like motorization. We provide transparent pricing during your free consultation.

Can I see out at night?You can see out, but people outside can also see in when interior lights are on. If nighttime privacy matters, consider pairing with another treatment or choosing a different option.

Do solar shades block heat?Yes, significantly. They block heat before it enters your home, which is much more effective than trying to remove heat after it's already inside.

What color should I choose?Darker colors provide better heat control and glare reduction. Lighter colors offer a clearer view and brighter aesthetic. We'll show you samples so you can decide what matters most.

Can I get them motorized?Absolutely. Motorization is popular with solar shades since you're adjusting them throughout the day based on sun position. Battery-powered options require no electrical work.

Do they come in different colors?Yes. While many people choose neutral colors, we offer various shades to complement your decor. The fabric color affects both aesthetics and performance.

What's the difference between solar shades and roller shades?Solar shades use mesh fabric designed specifically to block UV rays and heat while maintaining your view. Roller shades use opaque or semi-opaque fabric for privacy and light control but block your view when lowered.
