Blinds, Shades, and Window Treatment Solutions for New Haven, Indiana Homes

Energy-Efficient Cellular Shades

New Haven winters can be brutal, and those heating bills add up quickly. Cellular shades, also known as honeycomb shades, create insulating air pockets that keep your home warmer in winter and cooler during those sticky summer months. We've installed these in homes throughout New Haven, and homeowners consistently tell us they notice the difference in both comfort and energy costs.

Double-cell options work especially well for north-facing windows or rooms that always seem too cold in winter. If you're in one of the older homes near downtown, these can make a real difference in how your home feels without the expense of replacing windows.

Motorized Blinds and Shades

Life gets busy, especially for New Haven families juggling work in Fort Wayne and kids' activities at the schools. Motorized window treatments give you one less thing to worry about. Control your blinds and shades with your phone, a remote, or even voice commands if you have a smart home setup.

We've installed motorized shades for families with tall windows, lots of windows, or simply those who appreciate the convenience. Parents with young children especially love the cordless safety feature that comes standard with motorization.

Custom Plantation Shutters

Plantation shutters add timeless elegance to New Haven homes, whether you're in a classic ranch-style house or a newer two-story. They're durable, easy to clean, and provide excellent insulation and light control. We've found they're particularly popular in living rooms and dining rooms where homeowners want that polished, finished look.

Solar Shades and Roller Shades

If you appreciate clean, modern lines, solar shades and roller shades might be perfect for your New Haven home. Solar shades are excellent for blocking UV rays and reducing glare while maintaining your view, ideal for home offices or any room where you spend time during the day.

Roller shades come in countless fabric options, from sheer to blackout, so you can get exactly the light control you need for each room. They're sleek, functional, and work well in contemporary homes throughout the New Haven area.

Roman Shades

For homeowners who want the soft look of fabric with the functionality of a shade, Roman shades offer the best of both worlds. They add warmth and sophistication to bedrooms, living rooms, and dining areas. We can add thermal linings for extra insulation, something New Haven homeowners appreciate during those January cold snaps.
