Real wood blinds show natural grain and come in a range of stain and paint finishes. Compare samples with existing cabinets or trim in living rooms, dining rooms, and bedrooms. Cellular shades for comfort

The air pockets in cellular shades help slow heat transfer through the window. For a room that feels cold or receives strong sun, compare light-filtering and room-darkening fabrics, single- and double-cell designs, and the fit around the opening. An insulating shade can help with comfort, while the existing window and how you use the shade also affect performance.

Roller shades for a simple finish

Roller shades raise into a compact roll and lower to cover the glass. Compare a light-filtering fabric for everyday living areas with a room-darkening fabric for bedrooms. We can discuss mounting above the frame when keeping more glass clear is a priority, and check that the chosen material suits the room conditions.

Vertical blinds draw to the side so you can use the sliding door. We check the door handle, the direction it opens, and the space needed for the stacked vanes. Compare materials and operating options, especially if the door is used frequently or furniture sits close to the opening.

Can I match blinds to existing wood trim?

We can bring finish samples to compare in your room. Natural grain and existing stains vary, so seeing the sample beside the trim is more useful than trying to match an online image.

What should I consider for a sliding glass door?

Door direction, handle clearance, and the space available for the treatment when open all matter. We can compare vertical blinds and other wide-opening options during the visit.

Can I choose manual controls?

Yes. Tell us how you want to use the treatment and we can compare the available manual or motorized controls for that product. You do not need app control to have custom shades.

Can I arrange a visit outside weekday hours?

Call Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm. If those times are difficult, tell David what works for you and he will check whether another appointment time is available.
