Do I need to visit a showroom?

No. We bring window treatment samples to your home so you can compare them with the room and its light. Tell us which products or rooms you have in mind when booking.

What if my home is outside the map?

Call or send your address through the Contact page. The outline is a general guide; we can confirm whether a visit is available for your location and discuss any arrangements before scheduling.

Can I ask about cleaning or repairs?

Yes. Contact us with the type of treatment and the issue. Repair and cleaning enquiries need their own assessment, so please mention those when you get in touch.
