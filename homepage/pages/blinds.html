Choosing blinds for each room

The best blinds for your Fort Wayne home depend on privacy, sunlight, and how you use each space. You don't need the same material at every window.

Living and dining rooms

Wood or faux wood blinds let you tilt the slats to soften glare while keeping some daylight. The slats can stay partly open when you want light without a fully exposed window.

Kitchens and bathrooms

Faux wood or vinyl can be a better fit than real wood near damp areas. Consider cleaning, ventilation, and how close the window is to a sink or shower.

Bedrooms and offices

Talk through morning sun, street-facing windows, and screen glare. If a darker bedroom is your priority, compare blinds with room-darkening shades during your consultation.

How much do custom blinds cost?

Cost depends on window size, material, controls, and the number of windows. Aluminum and vinyl are often worth comparing when cost is a priority. We'll show you options at different price points and prepare a quote for your project during the free consultation.

Should I choose wood or faux wood blinds?

Wood offers a natural finish for living rooms, dining rooms, and bedrooms. Faux wood handles moisture better than real wood and is often a practical choice for kitchens and bathrooms. Compare samples in the room where the blinds will be installed.

Which blinds work for sliding glass doors?

Vertical blinds and panel track blinds are worth considering for sliding doors and wide openings. We'll check how the door opens, where the blinds stack, and the space available around the frame.

Do you measure and install the blinds?

Yes. We measure your windows, help you choose the material and controls, and return for professional installation. Ask us to go through the quote so you know what is included for your project.

How long does ordering and installation take?

Timing depends on the blinds you choose, manufacturer availability, and the size of your project. We will confirm the expected schedule when you order and arrange installation once your blinds are ready.

Can my existing blinds be repaired?

Some problems can be repaired without replacing the whole treatment. Tell us what has stopped working and, if possible, the brand. Visit our blind and shade repair page for help with the blinds you already have.
