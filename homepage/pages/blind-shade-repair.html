What is your blind
or shade doing?

The symptom is a useful place to start. The brand, model, age, and condition determine which repair options are available.

It will not raise or lower

Broken lift cords, worn cord locks, clutches, or internal mechanisms can affect operation. Ask about restringing and lift-system repairs for your particular blind or shade.

The slats will not tilt

A damaged wand, tilt mechanism, or connection may stop horizontal blinds from opening and closing. We check whether compatible replacement parts are available.

Something is bent or missing

Slats, vertical vanes, carriers, brackets, and tracks can wear or break. Matching the original size and finish matters, especially when replacing only part of a treatment.

Repair options for
blinds and shades

Different treatments use different mechanisms. We need to identify the product before confirming a repair.

Tell us what needs attention

Wood, faux wood, vinyl, and mini blinds

Ask about lift-cord restringing, cord locks, tilt gears, wands, and individual slats. For aluminum mini blinds or older wood blinds, the availability of matching parts may affect whether repair makes sense.

Vertical blinds

Trouble moving or rotating the vanes can come from the carriers, controls, or track. We can discuss damaged vanes and track components, including whether replacements will match the existing blind.

Roller and Roman shades

Roller shades may have spring or clutch problems. Roman shades can develop issues with lift cords, rings, or other operating parts. Fabric condition and the construction of the shade affect the repair options.

Cellular and honeycomb shades

Lift cords, cord locks, and internal parts can sometimes be repaired or replaced. Torn fabric, separated cells, and discontinued mechanisms need individual assessment; restringing alone will not resolve every problem.

Motorized blinds and shades

Describe whether the treatment fails to respond, stops partway, or works with one control but not another. Power, batteries, remotes, and motor compatibility need to be considered before assuming the motor has failed. Learn about motorized treatments.

Graber and other brands

Ask about Graber blind and shade repair, including treatments installed by another company. Have the brand and model available if possible. Repair depends on the mechanism, condition, and access to compatible parts.

Repair, clean,
or replace?

Choose based on the condition of the treatment and the work it needs, not just its age.

Repair a specific fault

A specific mechanical fault may be worth fixing when the rest of the blind or shade is in good condition and compatible parts are available.

Clean a dusty treatment

Dust or surface buildup calls for a different service than a broken mechanism. Explore blind and shade cleaning if the treatment operates properly but needs attention to its appearance.

Compare a replacement

Worn fabric, several damaged components, or unavailable parts may make replacement more practical. Compare custom blinds or custom shades if repair is not the right fit.

Can you repair blinds you did not install?

Ask us about your existing blinds or shades, even if another company installed them. We need the product details and condition to determine whether we can obtain suitable parts and carry out the repair.

Can broken blind cords be replaced?

Restringing may be possible for compatible mechanisms. The cord system, available parts, and overall condition matter. Do not try to force a stuck control; describe the issue when you call.

Do you repair cellular, roller, and Roman shades?

We can discuss repairs for these shade types, including lift and control problems. Availability depends on the specific construction and parts. Fabric damage may require a different solution from a mechanical repair.

Do you repair motorized blinds and shades?

Contact us with the brand, control type, and symptoms. A power or remote issue can look like a motor problem. We need to check the system and parts options before confirming the work.

How much does blind repair cost?

The fault, treatment type, number of units, parts, and labor affect the quote. Confirm any assessment or service charges and compare repair with replacement before deciding.

Can the repair be done in my home?

Some work can be completed on site; other repairs require parts or different arrangements. We confirm the service process and expected timing for your treatment. Same-day completion is not assumed.
