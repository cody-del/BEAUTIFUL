The details behind the fit

Measuring for your
windows and your room

Older frames, wide glass, and projecting handles can change the way a treatment needs to be mounted. We check those details before an order is placed.

Inside or outside the frame

Recess depth, frame condition, and uneven openings affect the mounting choice. We discuss how much trim stays visible and where the treatment will sit. In a bedroom, that also means considering light around the edges.

Space to lift, tilt, or open

We look at nearby handles, doors, furniture, and the space a raised or drawn treatment occupies. Shutter panels need room to swing; wide blinds and shades need a practical way to operate.

High windows and motorized controls

For hard-to-reach windows, ask about available motorized options. Product compatibility, control method, and power access are part of the discussion. Read about motorized blinds and shades.

Unusual shapes and new construction

Mention shaped windows or unfinished openings when you contact us. We can discuss suitable product options and when final measurements should be taken, including any work that needs to be completed first.

Choose the rooms

Care and adjustments

Cleaning methods vary by material. Follow the instructions for the product you selected, and ask before using a cleaner you are unsure about. If a blind or shade isn’t working properly, tell us what’s happening and we’ll help you work out the next step.

Warranty questions

We’ll explain the manufacturer’s warranty for the treatments you choose. If a problem comes up later, contact David with the product details so he can check the coverage and help with the next step.

Existing treatments need attention?

You may need a service for the treatments you already own. Explore blind and shade repair for operating problems, or blind and shade cleaning for dust and buildup. Availability and pricing depend on the work.

Before you book

A few answers about the consultation and installation process.

Talk with David
Is the in-home consultation free?

Yes. Contact us to arrange a free in-home consultation for new custom window treatments. Share your address and project details so we can confirm the visit. Repair and cleaning are separate services.

Should I measure my windows first?

You do not need final measurements before contacting us. A rough window count and a description of the rooms are a useful start. We take the measurements needed for your custom order.

Can I see samples before ordering?

Yes. We bring samples to your home so you can compare colors, textures, and privacy levels in your own lighting. You review the selected products and quote before placing the order.

How long does the process take?

Timing depends on product selection, manufacturing availability, and the installation schedule. Mention any project deadline when you call. We discuss the expected timeframe before ordering and arrange installation when the treatments arrive.

Can I request an evening or weekend visit?

Call us Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm. Need an evening or weekend visit? Let David know what works for you, and he’ll check his schedule.

Do you visit homes outside Fort Wayne?

We serve Fort Wayne, Allen County, and nearby communities. Check the service-area directory and contact us to confirm a visit for your address.
