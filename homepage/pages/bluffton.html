Will blackout fabric make the room completely dark?

It blocks light through the fabric, but gaps around the treatment can still admit light. We check the mounting and edge coverage and explain what to expect at your windows.

Do cellular shades replace the need to repair a drafty window?

They can help insulate at the window, but they do not repair a damaged frame or failed seal. Tell us about drafts so we can discuss the shade fit alongside the condition of the window.

Can all the treatments match from outside?

We can compare backing colors and finishes across the products you are considering. Tell us if a consistent street-facing appearance matters or if you have neighborhood requirements to follow.

How long does a custom order take?

Timing depends on the products and finishes you select. We will discuss the expected schedule with your quote and arrange installation when the treatments arrive.
