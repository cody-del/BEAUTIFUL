Privacy and sun control for each space

The same fabric will not suit every room. We compare coverage and material choices at the windows before ordering.

Blackout cellular shades for bedrooms

Cellular shades combine honeycomb air pockets with a choice of fabrics, including blackout material. The pockets help insulate at the window, while the fabric blocks light through the shade. Light can still enter around the edges. We discuss side coverage, mounting depth, and operation when planning a bedroom or a room used for daytime sleep.

Wood and faux wood blinds

Real wood blinds show natural grain and offer stain or paint choices to compare with your furniture and trim. Faux wood offers better moisture resistance for kitchens and bathrooms. Both provide adjustable slats for light control. We can explain the differences in weight and care and check which materials suit the size of your opening.

Solar shades for sunrooms and other bright spaces

Solar mesh can reduce glare and UV exposure while allowing a daytime view. Tell us whether the room is heated, exposed to moisture, or open to weather, especially for a porch, workshop, or outbuilding. Interior solar shades are not automatically suitable outdoors. We will discuss the setting before narrowing the product and fabric choices.

Roller shades for straightforward coverage

Roller shades provide a simple fabric surface with options ranging from light-filtering to blackout. Compare materials against the room’s light and consider how much of the glass you want clear when the shade is raised. For nighttime privacy, ask about an opaque fabric instead of relying on the daytime appearance of a sheer or solar shade.

Will blackout shades block every bit of light?

Blackout fabric blocks light through the material, but mounting gaps can still let light into the room. We discuss the edges and installation position so you know what to expect.

Can I use the same shade inside and on the porch?

It depends on the product and exposure. Indoor treatments are not automatically made for outdoor wind, moisture, or temperature conditions. Tell us about the porch so we can compare suitable options.

Do I need to order for the whole house?

No. We can start with bedrooms or other priority spaces and discuss the rest later. Give us your budget and window count so we can help plan the order.

Can I arrange an evening or weekend visit?

Appointments depend on availability. Call Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm, and let David know which times work for you. He can check the schedule before you make arrangements.
