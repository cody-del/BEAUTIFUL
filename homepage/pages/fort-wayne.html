Cellular shades for window comfort

Honeycomb pockets trap air at the window. Compare light-filtering and room-darkening fabrics, cell construction, and mounting options if a room feels uncomfortable near the glass. A close fit and how you operate the shade matter; a shade does not repair a draft or replace a damaged window.

Explore cellular shades

Solar and roller shades for bright rooms

A home office may need less glare while a bedroom needs more darkness. Solar screen fabrics offer different levels of openness and outward view. Roller shades offer light-filtering and room-darkening fabrics. We discuss nighttime privacy and light around the edges as well as the fabric itself.

Compare solar shades

Roman shades and fabric finishes

Roman shades bring a fabric finish to living rooms, dining rooms, and bedrooms. Look at texture and color beside your furnishings, then consider lining and the space taken up by raised folds. We check which fabrics and controls suit the opening before you order.

Explore Roman shades

Motorized blinds and shades

High great-room windows, shades behind furniture, and groups of windows can be easier to use with motorization. Ask about remote operation, optional app or smart-home compatibility, and battery or wired power for your selected product. We confirm the system and access needed before ordering.

Measuring for the windows you have

From older trim to large contemporary openings, we check the frame, handles, and available space before recommending how to mount the treatment.

Original trim and shallow frames

For an older window, we check the recess depth, projecting hardware, and the trim you want to keep visible. Inside and outside mounting change both the appearance and the coverage. If your windows will be replaced, let us know before final measurements.

Wide windows and doors

Picture windows, patio doors, and large panes need room for a treatment to stack or move. We look at handles, nearby furniture, access to the door, and the practical size limits of each product. Plantation shutter panels also need clearance to open.

New construction and renovation

Share your move-in or remodeling schedule when arranging a consultation. You can start by identifying priority rooms, then confirm measurements once the openings and trim are ready. We explain the expected product and installation timing with your quote.

Do I need to come to a showroom?

No. We bring samples to your Fort Wayne home for a free consultation. Comparing them at the window helps you judge color, texture, and privacy in the actual room.

Can I replace just one blind?

Yes. We can discuss one window or a larger project. If you want to coordinate with existing treatments, show us what is already installed so we can compare available finishes.

Should I choose the same treatment throughout the house?

You can coordinate colors while choosing different products for different rooms. A bedroom, patio door, and sunny office may need different fabrics, coverage, and controls.

Can you clean or repair the treatments I have?

Ask about our repair service or blind and shade cleaning. Tell us what you have and what needs attention so we can help you decide what to do next.
