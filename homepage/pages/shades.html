The right shade for the room

Color, texture, and your budget

Fabric texture, patterns, and linings change the appearance and price of a shade. Roller, pleated, cellular, and Roman styles offer different options. Motorization also affects your quote.

Kitchens and bathrooms

Think about humidity, splashes, and cleaning before choosing a fabric. Ask which materials are suitable near a sink or shower, and compare moisture-resistant blinds where a fabric shade may not be the right fit.

Large or unusual windows

For patio doors, skylights, arches, or angled windows, we check dimensions, access, and mounting space. The opening determines which shade styles and controls are practical.

How much do custom window shades cost?

The price depends on window size, fabric, style, liners, and controls. We compare options at different price points and prepare a quote for your project during the free consultation.

Are blackout shades completely dark?

Blackout fabric blocks light through the fabric, but light can enter around the edges or mounting hardware. We discuss fabric and installation options together, especially for bedrooms.

Do solar shades give privacy at night?

Do not rely on a solar screen for nighttime privacy. When the room is brighter than outside, people may be able to see in. Discuss a separate privacy treatment if that matters for your window.

Can I motorize my shades?

Motorization is available for selected shade styles and sizes. We check the product, power source, and control options for your windows before recommending a system.

What is the difference between cellular and pleated shades?

Cellular shades have enclosed honeycomb pockets. Pleated shades use folded fabric without that same cellular construction. Compare their light control, appearance, and insulating features in person.

Can you help with existing shades?

Yes. Contact us about shade repairs or cleaning. Tell us the type of shade and what needs attention so we can discuss the next step.
