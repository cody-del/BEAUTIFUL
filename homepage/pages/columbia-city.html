Cellular shades for window insulation

Cellular shades use pockets of air within the fabric to help slow heat transfer at the window. They are worth comparing for bedrooms and living areas where comfort is a priority. Choose light-filtering or room-darkening fabric and discuss single- or double-cell options. The shade, its fit, and the existing window all affect the result.

Explore cellular shades

Solar and roller shades for bright rooms

Solar mesh can reduce glare and UV exposure while allowing a daytime view. We compare fabric openness at the windows where sunlight reaches your seating or screens. Roller shades also come in privacy and blackout fabrics. If a room needs coverage after dark, consider an opaque fabric or a second layer rather than relying on solar mesh alone.

Explore roller shades

Roman shades for a softer finish

Roman shades bring fabric and texture to bedrooms, dining rooms, and living rooms. Compare patterns beside the furniture, then choose a fold style and lining. We also check how much of the window the fabric stack will cover when raised, especially where you want to preserve daylight or a view.

Can custom treatments fit older windows?

Yes. We measure each opening and check its depth, trim, and hardware. That lets us discuss suitable mounting choices before ordering instead of relying on a standard shelf size.

Should I choose wood or composite shutters?

Compare the finish, room conditions, and care requirements. Wood offers natural grain and stain choices; composite is an option for rooms with more moisture. We can explain the differences using samples.

Can I update just one room?

Yes. You can replace a few treatments now and plan other rooms later. Share your overall budget and priorities so we can prepare a quote that fits the current project.

Do I need measurements before calling?

No. An approximate window count and a description of the rooms are helpful, but we take the measurements for your custom order during your home visit.
