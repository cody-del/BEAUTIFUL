Before your consultation

A few useful things to know when planning new window coverings in Monroeville.

Talk with David
Do I need measurements before I call?

No. Tell us roughly how many windows you want to cover and mention any unusually high or wide openings. We take the measurements needed for the custom order during the visit.

Are faux wood blinds always the better choice?

The right choice depends on the opening and the room. Compare moisture resistance, finish, weight, and care requirements for the actual products. We can bring wood and faux wood samples so you can see the difference.

Can I replace treatments a room at a time?

We can discuss a single room or a larger project. Let us know your priorities, especially if you want new treatments to coordinate with ones you are keeping.

Can you look at an existing blind instead?

Call with the type of treatment and the problem. Our repair and cleaning pages explain those services. We can discuss whether either is appropriate before you plan a replacement.
