at fabric and function.

Bring the questions that matter in your home: a cold window, glare at the desk, a bedroom that needs darkness, or a shade you cannot reach.

Cellular shades and comfort

Cellular shades use pockets of air as an insulating layer at the window. Compare constructions, fabric opacity, and mounting options. The result depends on the window and selected shade; discuss product specifications rather than assuming a fixed energy saving.

Explore cellular shades

Solar screens and roller fabrics

A solar screen helps manage bright daylight with some outward view, while roller fabrics offer different privacy and room-darkening choices. Describe how you use the room after dark, too. A solar fabric by itself may not give you the privacy you need.

Solar shadesRoller shades

Roman shades and linings

Roman shades gather into fabric folds as they rise. Compare patterns beside your furnishings, ask which linings are compatible, and check the raised stack height. In a bedroom, the mounting arrangement matters alongside the lining.

Explore Roman shades

Prepare for a useful consultation.

You do not need to choose every product before speaking with David.

List the rooms

Note which treatments you are replacing and which you plan to keep. Mention whether coordinating with existing finishes is a priority.

Point out the obstacles

Deep handles, shallow frames, nearby furniture, and uneven openings all affect the fit. Rough photos or a description can help explain the project before the visit.

Discuss the new windows

For new construction, ask when final measurements should be taken. Discuss high openings and possible motorization before finishes make power access difficult.
