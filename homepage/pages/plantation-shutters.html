Fitting shutters to your windows

Frames and clearance

We check the frame depth, handles, locks, and nearby furniture. Panels need room to open, and louvers need room to tilt. Inside or outside mounting depends on the opening.

Privacy, sunlight, and moisture

Tilt the louvers to adjust daylight in living and dining rooms, or close them for more privacy in bedrooms. In kitchens and bathrooms, compare suitable moisture-resistant materials. Some light can still come through around the panels and louvers, so shutters won’t make a room completely dark.

Doors and specialty shapes

Arches, angled windows, and doors need individual planning. We check the opening and discuss which panel configurations and specialty shapes are available for your chosen material.

Answers about shutter materials, louver sizes, special shapes, and care.

Should I choose wood or composite shutters?

Wood offers natural character and a range of finishes for rooms without high moisture. Composite and poly products can be suitable for more humid spaces. We’ll help you compare the finishes, prices, and warranties for the shutters that suit your room.

How much do custom plantation shutters cost?

Window dimensions, material, panel configuration, specialty shapes, and installation details affect the quote. We measure your windows and explain your options during the free in-home consultation.

Which louver size is best?

The right size depends on your window depth, view, and preferred appearance. We can compare 2½, 3½, and 4½-inch sizes where the product allows them and show how the louvers clear your window hardware.

Can shutters work on doors or arched windows?

Some shutter systems accommodate doors, arches, or angled openings. We’ll measure the opening and check which shapes and panel layouts will fit.

How long does it take to get shutters installed?

Manufacturing and installation timing varies by product and project. We confirm the expected schedule when you order and arrange installation once your shutters are ready.

How do I clean and care for shutters?

Dust regularly and follow the manufacturer’s instructions for your material and finish. Ask before using water or cleaning products on wood. We’ll show you how to care for your shutters and explain their warranty.
