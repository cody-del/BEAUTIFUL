Can I keep a lake view while reducing glare?

Solar mesh is one option to compare. Openness, fabric color, and the light outside affect the view. We can bring samples so you can judge the tradeoff at your own window.

Will every motorized shade connect to my phone?

No. Available features depend on the selected motor and control system. App or remote access may require additional accessories and a connection at the property; we will explain the setup before ordering.

Are shutters suitable for a humid room?

Material matters. Composite shutters offer greater moisture resistance than wood, but we still need to check the room conditions and product guidance. Let us know about humidity or seasonal temperature changes.
